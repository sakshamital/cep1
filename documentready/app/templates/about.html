About DocumentReady

An independent platform to help citizens prepare document checklists before applying for government services.

What is DocumentReady?

DocumentReady is a community engagement project (AI & Data Science, UG 2026–27) that helps citizens understand what documents they need to gather before visiting a government office to apply for a service, certificate, or scheme.

The core feature is a document readiness checker: you select a service, mark which documents you have and which are missing, and the system tells you exactly what to obtain first — with step-by-step guidance.

How It Works

  1. Search for a service (e.g. "Caste Validity Certificate")
  2. The system shows a complete document checklist with all required, optional and conditional items
  3. Mark each item Available or Missing
  4. For missing documents, tap "How to obtain?" for a step-by-step guide — which may itself have prerequisites
  5. When all required documents are ready, click Verify Checklist to confirm
  6. You see: "You're Ready to Apply."

Official Verification & Advisory Notice

{{ disclaimer }}

All requirements and document checklists on DocumentReady are cross-verified against official state and central portals. Each service includes direct links to official portals (such as Aaple Sarkar, CCVIS/BARTI, UIDAI, and MahaDBT) and records the latest verification timestamp.

This platform is an independent readiness support tool designed to guide citizens through prerequisites before visiting government offices.

Data Policy

  • We never ask for Aadhaar numbers, document numbers, names, phone numbers or file uploads
  • We only store your checklist status (Available/Missing per document) in your browser's localStorage
  • No data is sent to or stored on any server
  • Clearing your browser's localStorage removes all checklist data

Technology Stack

Built with Python / Flask (backend), plain HTML5 + CSS3 + vanilla JavaScript (frontend), and SQLite database with JSON schema validation. No external CDN dependencies — works fully offline on localhost.

Verification & Continuous Monitoring

Our verification pipeline ensures document requirements remain accurate:

  • All service checklists are cross-referenced with official gazettes and department circulars
  • Issuing authorities, office modes, fees and timelines are validated against live government portals
  • Automated source monitoring tracks policy changes in real-time
  • Every verified update is timestamped and published to the public updates feed
